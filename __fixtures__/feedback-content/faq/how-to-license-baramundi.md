# How do I license baramundi Management Suite?

**URL:** https://feedback.baramundi.com/faq/licensing
**Published:** 2023-06-15
**Category:** Licensing

---

baramundi Management Suite uses a **node-based licensing model** where you purchase licenses based on the number of managed endpoints.

## License Types

### 1. baramundi Management Suite (Core)
- Includes: Software deployment, OS deployment, patch management
- Per-endpoint licensing
- Available in tiers: 100, 250, 500, 1000, 2500, 5000+ endpoints

### 2. Optional Modules
- **Mobile Device Management (MDM)**: iOS/Android management
- **Apple VPP**: Volume Purchase Program integration
- **Cloud Management Gateway**: Internet-based endpoint management

## Licensing Process

1. **Purchase**: Contact sales or authorized reseller
2. **License File**: Receive license file (.lic) via email
3. **Import**: Management Console → Administration → License Management
4. **Activation**: License activates automatically (requires internet)

## Trial Licenses

- 30-day full-featured trial available
- Download from: https://www.baramundi.com/trial
- Converts to full license when purchased (no reinstall needed)
